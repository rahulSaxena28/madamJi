Before I ask

What you are to me

तुम बिन जीना मुमकिन नहीं I cannot live without you

Some people spend their whole lives looking for home. I found mine in you.

You are my first thought in the morning and my last one before I sleep. You are the laughter when my day is heavy and the quiet when everything is too loud. Every good part of me grew because you believed in it first.

I have tried to picture a life without you, and I can’t. There is no version of me that makes sense alone.

You are not a part of my life. You are my whole life.

You are my

peace

From first light to forever

I want every moment with you

Not the big days only. All of them, from the minute your eyes open to the years we haven’t lived yet.

  1. 5:30 AM

    The first light

    I want to wake up and find you there, sleepy and warm, before the world asks anything of us.

  2. 9:00 AM

    Ordinary mornings

    Chai in your hands, mine going cold on the table, and nothing urgent to say. Just us.

  3. 1:00 PM

    The middle of a busy day

    Call me with nothing to tell me. I will listen to all of it. Every day is lighter when I know you are somewhere in it.

  4. 6:30 PM

    Golden hour

    Slow walks, long drives, and the songs we both pretend we don’t know the words to.

  5. 11:00 PM

    Late at night

    Your head on my shoulder, the lights low, and every worry of yours mine to carry for a while.

  6. Always

    Every year after

    Grey hair, slower steps, the same hand in mine. I will still look at you like it is the first day.

What I promise you

I will choose you every morning, even on the hard days.

I will hold your hand in the quiet and in the noise.

I will make you laugh on the days you forget how.

I will love you more tomorrow than I do today.

So here is my question

Will you spend forever with me?

Again, and every day after this one.

You said yes.

Thank you for choosing me again. I will spend every day trying to deserve you.
